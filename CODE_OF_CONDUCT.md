# Code of Conduct

GovernSpec aims to be a respectful and practical open-source project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent, while staying clear about technical risks.
- Focus feedback on code, documentation, tests, and reproducible behavior.
- Welcome newcomers and help them understand the project context.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion after maintainers ask to refocus.

## Reporting

If you need to report a conduct concern, contact the maintainer at
`research@symboliclight.com`. Reports will be handled with care and, where possible,
confidentiality.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when needed
to keep the project safe and productive.
